01 — Journey

From Agency Innovation to AI-First Products

My career has been a progression from understanding search algorithms to building the algorithms themselves—and crafting the experiences that make them useful. I started in the agency world, leading the SEO practice at 360i—the #1 search marketing agency recognized by Forrester—where I served Fortune 100 brands reaching over 430 million searchers annually.

That foundation in understanding how technology serves customer needs led me to BrightEdge, where I shifted from consultant to builder. I developed scalable automation products, built internal tools with modern stacks, and learned to ship software that customers actually used—growing one product 5x in installs and MRR over 6 months.

Today at Profitero+, I'm doing what I love most: leading cross-functional teams to build AI-first products from zero to one. Over three years, I've worked daily with frontend developers and product designers to stand up a cohesive B2B application supporting complex workflows with intuitive UX. I took our Content Optimizer from concept to 100+ enterprise customers, scaled a team of 24 engineers and 7 product professionals, and pioneered the integration of LLMs into our product architecture since GPT-3.

The thread throughout? Equal parts technical depth and design thinking—the ability to translate complex technical capabilities into products that feel effortless to use. Whether prototyping in Figma, collaborating in code reviews, or finding creative solutions to technical constraints, I bridge the gap between what's possible and what's delightful.
